01 / THE WORKFLOW

Less retyping.
More checking.

For the person entering wholesale orders into billing software. ParchiMatch Lite works from pasted text and your shop’s own catalogue.

Paste the order

Hinglish, Hindi number words and Devanagari. Keep the shorthand your shop uses.

Match the catalogue

Code reads the numbers. Text matching goes first; a local model helps only when needed.

Answer the amber rows

Confirm a suggestion, make a correction, or skip it. The original line stays in view.

Export a checked CSV

Download a CSV for manual import into your billing software. Skipped items stay in the export.

02 / SAFE BY DESIGN

A wrong row must never be auto-accepted.

The design principle: when unsure, ask one short question.

Read all 16 safety rules →
  • A written price must match the pack.

    “parle g 10 wala” cannot quietly become a ₹5 pack.

  • Pack sizes must match, including the unit family.

    “fortune 5 litre” cannot become a 1 L pouch.

  • Unknown units become a question.

    “20 jodi” → What is “jodi”? packet or carton?

  • Two quantities need a clear explanation.

    “maggi 2 ctn 5 pkt” → Which quantity?

  • A carton needs a known carton size.

    “clinic plus 2 petti” → How many in one carton?

03 / ON THE SHOP’S COMPUTER

Local by design.
Offline in use.

The app, catalogue and model run on the same machine. No cloud inference, telemetry or third-party scripts. After installation, it works with Wi-Fi off.

The local server binds to 127.0.0.1 only. Without Ollama, the app still runs fuzzy matching.

07 / WHAT TO KNOW FIRST

Useful boundaries.
Stated plainly.

The current scope

  • Evaluated on synthetic data only.
  • No real shop has used it yet.
  • One shop, one operator, one order at a time.
  • Pasted text only. No photos, voice or WhatsApp integration.
  • A comma inside an item’s description, such as “maggi, 4 pack 1 ctn”, splits it into two rows. Commas inside numbers (“1,500”) are read correctly.
  • The local server has no authentication; it is a single-user desktop tool.
